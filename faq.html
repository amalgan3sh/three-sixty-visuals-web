FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What types of photography do you offer?

We specialize in wedding, modeling, event, save the date, and automobile photography. If you have a unique request, feel free to contact us!

How do I book a session?

You can book a session by contacting us via our website, email, or phone. We recommend booking in advance to secure your preferred date.

Where are you based and do you travel?

We are based in Birmingham, UK, and are available for travel across the UK and beyond for special projects and events.

How soon will I receive my photos?

Our standard turnaround time is 2-4 weeks, depending on the type of session. We always aim to deliver your photos as quickly as possible without compromising quality.

Can I request specific shots or styles?

Absolutely! We encourage clients to share their ideas and preferences so we can tailor the session to your vision.
